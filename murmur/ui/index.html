How to dictate

Click into any text box, hold the keys, say your thing, let go. Your words appear where the cursor is. Every recording is saved safely first, so nothing gets lost if something goes wrong.

Words to listen for

Names, clients, technical terms. This nudges recognition towards them.

Always replace

If Murmur writes something you'd rather it didn't, fix it for good.

Voice commands

Say “new line” or “new paragraph” (also “neue Zeile”, “neuer Absatz”) and Murmur types the break.

Say “scratch that” (or “streich das”) to remove what Murmur just pasted. This only works right after pasting, in the same window, and only if you haven't typed since.

German tax vocabulary

About 130 terms (Umsatzsteuer, DATEV, BFH, GoBD, …). Helps recognition and fixes spelling, for example “datev” becomes “DATEV”.

Snippets

Say the trigger phrase on its own (or “insert” plus the trigger) and Murmur pastes the saved text exactly as written.

Per-app behaviour

Choose how Murmur writes in different programs. Writing styles use your local Ollama model (see “Text polish”).

Updates (USB stick or folder)

Copy a signed .murmurupdate file to a USB stick. When Murmur sees it, it checks the signature and offers to install it. No internet needed. Murmur never installs anything that isn't signed with the owner's key.

Interface language

Text polish (optional, local AI)

Tidies punctuation and wording with a model running in your own Ollama on this computer. Nothing leaves your PC; if anything goes wrong, your original text is used.

Offline / office mode

Blocks every network connection this app tries to make to anything except your own computer. Model downloads are switched off; import models from a folder or network drive instead.

Tip: download a model once on any PC, then copy the folder %APPDATA%\Murmur\models to a USB stick or network drive.

Audio safety

Audio is written to disk while you speak and the file stays valid even if the app crashes. Interrupted recordings are recovered on the next launch and transcribed into History. Murmur never deletes audio that failed to transcribe.

Keep finished audio

After that, audio moves to “Recently deleted” for 14 more days before it's gone. The text stays in History. Star a clip to keep its audio forever.
