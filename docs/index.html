Talk. Murmur types.

Hold a key, speak, let go. Your words appear wherever your cursor is. Free, private, and it all runs on your own PC.

Download for Windows
Free · Windows 10/11 · about 110 MB · no account

🔒 Private by design

Speech is transcribed on your computer with open-source Whisper. Your voice never leaves your machine.

🪶 Never loses a word

Audio is saved to disk while you speak. If anything crashes, the recording is recovered next launch.

🏢 Offline mode for the office

One switch blocks every non-local connection. Load models from a USB stick or network drive.

🌍 English, German and more

Auto-detects the language. Teach it your own names and jargon. Interface in English or German.

⌨️ Works everywhere

Email, Word, Slack, your browser: anywhere you can type. Hold Ctrl+Win to talk.

Get started in a minute

  1. Download and unzip the folder anywhere.
  2. Open Murmur.exe. Murmi lives in your system tray (bottom right).
  3. Pick a speech model. Small is a great start. It downloads once.
  4. Click into any text box, hold Ctrl+Win, speak, release.

Questions

Windows says “unknown publisher” or SmartScreen blocks it

Murmur is a free hobby app and isn't paid-code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway. The source code is public if you want to check it.

Is it really free and offline?

Yes. After the one-time model download Murmur needs no internet. In offline mode even the download is disabled.

Where are my recordings?

In %APPDATA%\Murmur. The History tab lets you replay, copy, re-transcribe and keep clips forever.
